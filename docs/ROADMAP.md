# Roadmap to live (target 15 December)

**Stage 1 · done.** Next.js + React app; all main screens in React; 502 Google-facing pages; sitemap; API route skeleton; Playwright tests; CI.

**Stage 2 · React sheets and polish.** Search-engine groundwork first (done: full server HTML on every Google-facing page, crawlable links, canonicals, structured data, sitemap with planned trips, Search Console/Bing/GA4 hooks). Done: every bottom sheet in React; day plans on home and island pages; vehicle chip and scooter-for-the-day; "Add a day before/after"; quick hotel compare; pairing suggestions after adding. Hindi on the main screens (navigation, home, trip, trip tools, every sheet, add panel, checkout). Light-mode QA done (contrast scan of every main page at 360px and 1440px). Stage 2 is complete. (Drag-to-move works through the v1 engine and is left as it is for now.)

**Stage 3 · Real bookings.** Supabase: done for bookings (tables, Row Level Security, `create_booking`, checkout saves the trip, webhook marks it paid); next: prices from `price_overrides`, partner accounts, phone-OTP login, Razorpay live payments + webhook, WhatsApp and email vouchers, server-side price check before payment.

**Stage 4 · Live data.** Ferry operator APIs through `/api/ferries/availability`, hotel allotments, partner inbox and gate scanner as real staff screens.

**Stage 5 · Launch.** Domain, verify in Google Search Console and Bing and submit the sitemap, Sentry error alerts, uptime checks, privacy policy (DPDP Act), test round with real phones.

## Rebuild to the approved design (started 20 Nov 2026)

The new design (phone-first, categorised browsing, boarding-pass ferry tiles, optional meals out, one checkout) was approved on a clickable prototype. The app is being rebuilt to it in small pull requests, each one safe to ship on its own.

**R1 · Trust, photos and names · done.** Company details (CIN, GSTIN, DPIIT number, registered office, email) and the six recognition logos in the site footer and schema.org data; booking terms, refunds and privacy pages (`/terms`, `/refunds`, `/privacy`, drafts for a lawyer to check); hotels' own photos on stay pages and in the stays list (57 of 178 so far; the rest show one labelled sample photo); our own heading on all 96 itineraries.

**R2 · Design tokens and shell · done.** The approved colours, type, spacing and corners are the app's tokens (`src/styles/tide.css`, loaded after `app.css`); the screens not yet rebuilt read them through their old names, so the whole app is already in the approved colours. Header with the logo, name and DPIIT line; four-tab dock (Explore, Trip, My trips, Profile) on the bottom edge, with the total-and-action bar joined to it; on wide screens the tabs sit in the header. Day is the default theme; Night and the language are chosen in Profile. New app icon. Left for later: the header's Sign in (comes with phone login, Stage 3) and the island colours (change with the Trip page, R4).

**R3 · Home and browsing · done.** Home: photo hero with the island clock and sunset, the plan box (search, dates, travellers, "Plan my trip", your trip so far), Book one thing, islands, ready-made trips, most loved, day plans, the islands today (sunrise, sunset, moon and glowing plankton worked out for the date), why trips hold together. Things to do, stays and ready-made trips share one browse screen: island or trip-length switch, search, category tiles, one short row per category, "See all" for a sorted list; every choice is in the address, so Back works and a link can be shared. Ferries: the main triangle as route cards, sailings grouped by time of day. Cabs: by island and kind of ride, with scooters. Island pages: facts, getting there, things to do, day plans, stays. Detail pages for a thing to do (how the day goes, included, not included, bring), a stay (gallery, rooms) and a ready-made trip (day by day with kind tiles). All of it is in the server HTML. The site is English only: the Hindi switch is gone. Left for later: the search page and the add-to-trip panel keep their old layout until R4.

**R4 · Trip · done.** The Trip page in the approved design. Cover with dates and travellers; a panel that names what does not fit before you open the fixes; where you sleep each night; a day rail with a mark on days that clash. Each day: a photo banner when the island changes, how full the day is, then every booking as a row with its kind tile and a panoramic photo for sights and adventures. Cabs are shown as loops (one car from the hotel, round the stops and back, one price, the rides inside on a tap). Ferries are boarding passes with a tear-off stub; a tap opens class and seats (the engine's own cabin layout, with what each class costs more). Eating out is optional: a chip per meal, timed into the day, paid at the table, with what a waiting cab would cost from the catalogue's waiting rates. Free time shows day plans and single stops that fit. On wide screens the summary stays in view beside the days.

**R5 · Checkout and after · done.** Checkout in the approved design. A strip with the trip and "prices checked at"; **who's travelling** (every name, age and gender when the trip has a ferry, because the operators need the passenger list; one contact when it has none; passport numbers for foreign nationals); the **price** by kind of booking, each line with its partner, GST by rate, the total and the price a person; **if plans change**, worked out from each booking's own cancellation rule, with the real date until which everything comes back in full; **pay with** UPI, card or netbanking; a company GSTIN for business trips; a plain notice that no money is taken until Razorpay is connected. Missing details are named in red and the page scrolls to the first one. After Pay, each partner ticks off in turn, then the confirmation: "You're going to the Andamans", what was paid, what is first up, share on WhatsApp, and the whole trip as a calendar file. **My trips** is the booked trip day by day: ferries as boarding passes, the day's cab rides as one row, a tap on any booking shows its code; saved itineraries below. The add-to-trip panel, every bottom sheet (now with a close button, and focus returns to where it was) and the search page (results in their groups, five at a time) are in the same design, so no screen is left in the old one.

**R6 · Plan, fold, share, ask · done.** Asked for after the rebuild was complete.
- **Home leads with one action.** "Plan my trip" in three taps (when, who, pace) builds the whole trip. `/plan` is the same builder with the kind of trip and the class of stay, in the new design.
- **Long trips open as one line a day.** Trips of more than three days show each day as a summary (what happens, how many plans, from when to when, the cost). A tap opens the day; the first day and any day that needs a fix start open; "Open all days" is one tap.
- **Sharing opens the plan on the site.** "Planning with others?" makes a link to `/trip/shared`. The plan travels inside the link (after the `#`), so nothing is uploaded and no name or number is in it. The page is inside the site, on the Trip tab: the days, times and prices, a comment button for each day that opens WhatsApp, and "Open it in my planner", which makes it the reader's own trip. With the database this becomes a short link.
- **Ask on WhatsApp everywhere.** Every activity, stay, ready-made trip, ferry route, cabs, each island, the browse screens, Trip, Checkout, My trips (and each booking in it), search with no results, and the footer open a chat with +91 99332 70842 with the question already written: what it is about, the traveller's dates and group, the booking code where there is one, and the page's address.
- **Maps.** Google's own map, shown only when asked for: where a stay or an activity is, each island, and on the Trip page each day's stops in order with one tap to directions in Google Maps (by Google place id where the catalogue has one).
- **Warm paper instead of pure white.** Page, cards and sunk areas are sand tones in day and warm charcoal at night; ink and coral are unchanged and every text colour is still at least 4.5:1.
- **Google.** Questions and answers worked out from the timetable on every ferry route and island page (with FAQPage data), the company phone and contact point in the organisation data, a 1200×630 JPEG for link previews, pictures in the sitemap, `lang="en-IN"`, and sharper titles for stays, activities, cabs and the planner.
- **Moving a plan is never a drag.** Sliding a row used to shift it by accident, and even a tap on Move could nudge the time. The Move button now only opens the sheet: pick the day, pick from every time the plan can run (times that clash are greyed out with the reason), or type a time where the plan is not tied to set departures, then press Move. Nothing changes until then.
- **Ferries are booked by our team.** For now every ferry ticket is booked by us within 15 minutes of the traveller's confirmation. The boarding pass looks the same; it says so, and there is no seat picking: we ask the traveller directly for seats. Checkout, the confirmation, My trips and the ferry pages all say the same thing.
- **Convenience fee.** A fee on the bookings we make, shown as an amount with what it pays for (we manage everything on the ground), with GST on the fee. It is inside every total from the Trip page on, including the ready-made trip prices, so nothing is added at checkout. The rate lives in one place in the engine (`FEE_RATE`).
- **More like this** never shows a thing twice, puts other things at the same place first, keeps places on their island, and lets activities you can do on several islands fill up from other islands.
- **Signature experiences** are back on Home, and "Hire a whole boat" is a photo tile.
- **Still to do from the same list:** real payments, the database and login; live seats from partners; change or cancel from My trips; installable and offline; ferry disruption. Charters, Profile and "change a booking" keep older markup under the new colours.

**Launch mode · Confirm first (this change).** How the site goes live before online payment is connected.
- **Checkout sends the trip for confirmation.** Nothing is paid on the site. "Send for confirmation" opens WhatsApp to +91 99332 70842 with a complete message: a reference, dates, route, what is in the trip, the total, every traveller, the contact, and a link that opens the exact plan on the site. Email is the fallback. Nothing is booked or posted to the server.
- **What the traveller is promised**, in the same words everywhere (`src/components/Promises.tsx`): confirmed in 30 minutes; the best price in India, because we are in Port Blair and deal with every partner ourselves with no middleman from the mainland; no sales calls; with you all the way. It is a band on Home, a box at the top of checkout, the screen after sending, a line under every price and in the footer.
- **After sending**: "Press Send in WhatsApp and you are done", then the four steps with the times. The request stays on the phone (it survives a reload), My trips shows it as waiting with the same reference, and sending a changed trip keeps the reference.
- **A ready message for every thing.** Under every add-to-trip panel: "Just this? Confirm it on WhatsApp", written from what is chosen on screen (a stay: hotel, check-in, nights, room; an activity: day and time; a ferry: operator, sailing, class and a question about seats; a cab: route, time, vehicle). Ready-made trips have their own.
- The booking terms say the same: confirmed before payment.
- **To switch online payment back on later**: the Pay step replaces "Send for confirmation" in `src/views/Checkout.tsx`; the tickets in My trips, the booking API and its checks are unchanged and still tested.

Adding hotel photos: put `<hotel id in lower case>-0.webp` … (720×480) and `-t.webp` (224×168) in `public/img/h` and set the count in `src/data/hotelphotos.json`.
