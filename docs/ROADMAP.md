# Roadmap to live (target 15 December)

**Stage 1 · done.** Next.js + React app; all main screens in React; 502 Google-facing pages; sitemap; API route skeleton; Playwright tests; CI.

**Stage 2 · React sheets and polish.** Search-engine groundwork first (done: full server HTML on every Google-facing page, crawlable links, canonicals, structured data, sitemap with planned trips, Search Console/Bing/GA4 hooks). Done: every bottom sheet in React; day plans on home and island pages; vehicle chip and scooter-for-the-day; "Add a day before/after"; quick hotel compare; pairing suggestions after adding. Hindi on the main screens (navigation, home, trip, trip tools, every sheet, add panel, checkout). Light-mode QA done (contrast scan of every main page at 360px and 1440px). Stage 2 is complete. (Drag-to-move works through the v1 engine and is left as it is for now.)

**Stage 3 · Real bookings.** Supabase: done for bookings (tables, Row Level Security, `create_booking`, checkout saves the trip, webhook marks it paid); next: prices from `price_overrides`, partner accounts, phone-OTP login, Razorpay live payments + webhook, WhatsApp and email vouchers, server-side price check before payment.

**Stage 4 · Live data.** Ferry operator APIs through `/api/ferries/availability`, hotel allotments, partner inbox and gate scanner as real staff screens.

**Stage 5 · Launch.** Domain, verify in Google Search Console and Bing and submit the sitemap, Sentry error alerts, uptime checks, privacy policy (DPDP Act), test round with real phones.

## Rebuild to the approved design (started 20 Nov 2026)

The new design (phone-first, categorised browsing, boarding-pass ferry tiles, optional meals out, one checkout) was approved on a clickable prototype. The app is being rebuilt to it in small pull requests, each one safe to ship on its own.

**R1 · Trust, photos and names · done.** Company details (CIN, GSTIN, DPIIT number, registered office, email) and the six recognition logos in the site footer and schema.org data; booking terms, refunds and privacy pages (`/terms`, `/refunds`, `/privacy`, drafts for a lawyer to check); hotels' own photos on stay pages and in the stays list (57 of 178 so far; the rest show one labelled sample photo); our own heading on all 96 itineraries.

**R2 · Design tokens and shell · done.** The approved colours, type, spacing and corners are the app's tokens (`src/styles/tide.css`, loaded after `app.css`); the screens not yet rebuilt read them through their old names, so the whole app is already in the approved colours. Header with the logo, name and DPIIT line; four-tab dock (Explore, Trip, My trips, Profile) on the bottom edge, with the total-and-action bar joined to it; on wide screens the tabs sit in the header. Day is the default theme; Night and the language are chosen in Profile. New app icon. Left for later: the header's Sign in (comes with phone login, Stage 3) and the island colours (change with the Trip page, R4).

**R3 · Home and browsing · done.** Home: photo hero with the island clock and sunset, the plan box (search, dates, travellers, "Plan my trip", your trip so far), Book one thing, islands, ready-made trips, most loved, day plans, the islands today (sunrise, sunset, moon and glowing plankton worked out for the date), why trips hold together. Things to do, stays and ready-made trips share one browse screen: island or trip-length switch, search, category tiles, one short row per category, "See all" for a sorted list; every choice is in the address, so Back works and a link can be shared. Ferries: the main triangle as route cards, sailings grouped by time of day. Cabs: by island and kind of ride, with scooters. Island pages: facts, getting there, things to do, day plans, stays. Detail pages for a thing to do (how the day goes, included, not included, bring), a stay (gallery, rooms) and a ready-made trip (day by day with kind tiles). All of it is in the server HTML. The site is English only: the Hindi switch is gone. Left for later: the search page and the add-to-trip panel keep their old layout until R4.

**R4 · Trip (this change).** The Trip page in the approved design. Cover with dates and travellers; a panel that names what does not fit before you open the fixes; where you sleep each night; a day rail with a mark on days that clash. Each day: a photo banner when the island changes, how full the day is, then every booking as a row with its kind tile and a panoramic photo for sights and adventures. Cabs are shown as loops (one car from the hotel, round the stops and back, one price, the rides inside on a tap). Ferries are boarding passes with a tear-off stub; a tap opens class and seats (the engine's own cabin layout, with what each class costs more). Eating out is optional: a chip per meal, timed into the day, paid at the table, with what a waiting cab would cost from the catalogue's waiting rates. Free time shows day plans and single stops that fit. On wide screens the summary stays in view beside the days. Left for later: the add-to-trip panel, the move and fix sheets and the search page keep their old layout until R5.

**R5 · Checkout and after.** Checkout with the price breakdown per partner, "if plans change", payment method, confirming and confirmed screens, My trips.
